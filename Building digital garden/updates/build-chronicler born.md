## Problem

## Why this way

**Intention:** Documentation as a product surface—if you can’t explain it plainly, you haven’t decided it.  
**Why:** Dogfooding our own “linked knowledge” idea for the build itself.

## What I learned

Write the story while still in the mess; waiting for “v1” loses the why behind workarounds.
